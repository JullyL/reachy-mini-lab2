Appendix D Data dictionary

The canonical analysis file is study/final_submission/analysis/analysis_ready.csv. It contains eight participant-by-condition rows, with one A and one B row for P01–P04. Blank or NA means unobserved, not zero. No HRIES items are imputed; pairs require both conditions. Raw sources remain unchanged.

Field or field group | Definition and coding

participant_id | De-identified participant P01–P04

gender and age_band | Workbook gender; register age band 18–24

robot_experience | Register category none or prior experience

reachy_experience | Register category yes/no; all no in this pilot

agreement | Register participation agreement yes/no

session_date and operator | From participants.csv; date 2026-09-30, facilitator/operator name

condition | A = 10°; B = 30° maximum displacement per antenna

antenna_amplitude_deg | Reported command setting 10.0 or 30.0 degrees

order and greeting_number | AB/BA and presentation position 1/2

study_version and survey_version | antenna-greeting-v1 and HRIES-greeting-v1

soc_1–soc_4 | Warm, Likeable, Trustworthy, Friendly; integer 1–7

ani_1–ani_4 | Alive, Natural, Real, Human-like; integer 1–7

age_1–age_4 | Self-reliant, Rational, Intentional, Intelligent; integer 1–7. age here means agency, not participant age

dis_1–dis_4 | Creepy, Scary, Uncanny, Weird; integer 1–7

sociability, animacy, agency, disturbance | Mean of the respective four items only when all four exist; higher disturbance means more disturbance

valence | 1 very negative; 4 neutral; 7 very positive

arousal | 1 very low activation; 4 moderate; 7 very high activation

movement_size | 1 very small; 7 very large; administered last

qualitative_response | Verbatim workbook wording, including original apostrophe typography

completion | 1 recorded completed; 0 interrupted/failed; missing if unobserved

completion_source | Recorded worksheet outcome; physical logs unavailable

failure_interruption_reason | Raw free text; blank where not recorded

protocol_deviation | deviations.csv; none recorded for all eight records

Output files add explicit labels: condition_summary.csv uses mean, sample_sd, median, and n; B_minus_A denotes paired differences. paired_differences.csv records A, B, and B_minus_A for each participant and measure. completion_summary.csv uses recorded_trials as its denominator. qualitative_coding.csv adds the descriptive category; audit.json records source hashes, missingness, and verification limits.
