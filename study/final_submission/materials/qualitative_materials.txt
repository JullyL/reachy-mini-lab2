Appendix E Qualitative responses and coding rules

Responses below reproduce the raw questionnaire workbook in recorded presentation order. One descriptive category is assigned per response. These are short accounts of a greeting rather than a validated qualitative measure.

P01   Condition A   Robotic or mechanical impression

“The tone was very flat, and the speaking pace and transitions between words were smooth and slow. It did not feel very enthusiastic.”

P01   Condition B   Increased liveliness or interactivity

“The tone was slightly more upbeat.”

P02   Condition B   Robotic or mechanical impression

“It felt robotic, cold, and mechanical.”

P02   Condition A   Little or no perceived difference

“It did not feel very different from the previous one.”

P03   Condition A   Robotic or mechanical impression

“It should interact with me more. The tone sounded quite robotic.”

P03   Condition B   Increased liveliness or interactivity

“It felt more interactive, and the speaking pace seemed a little faster, although I’m not sure if that was just my own perception.”

P04   Condition B   Little or no perceived difference

“I didn’t have any particular impression.”

P04   Condition A   Little or no perceived difference

“I didn’t have any particular impression.”

Category definitions

Robotic or mechanical impression: explicit descriptions of flat, robotic, cold, mechanical, or unenthusiastic behavior. P01 A, P02 B, and P03 A; 3 responses. P03 A also requests greater interaction, but its observed greeting description is robotic.

Increased liveliness or interactivity: explicit descriptions of a more upbeat or interactive greeting. P01 B and P03 B; 2 responses. P03’s perceived faster speech and uncertainty are retained in the full response, rather than removed from the interpretation.

Little or no perceived difference: an explicit lack of difference or lack of a particular impression. P02 A and P04 A/B; 3 responses. This category combines two kinds of weak response and should not be interpreted as proof that no perceptual difference occurred.
