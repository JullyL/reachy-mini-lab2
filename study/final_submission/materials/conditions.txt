Appendix A Conditions and implementation

Study version antenna-greeting-v1. The parameter antenna_amplitude_deg is maximum displacement from calibrated neutral per antenna. These are reported study settings; the archived physical verification record remains pending.

Property | Condition A | Condition B

Head target and base yaw | Identity pose and 0 rad | Identity pose and 0 rad

Greeting exposure | One completed greeting | One completed greeting

Neutral reset between conditions | 30 s after questionnaire | 30 s after questionnaire

In both conditions, the participant first says “Hello”; the facilitator then triggers the prepared robot response. The six-second cycle measures the robot’s response, excluding the participant’s initiation and operator delay. The exact WAV is assets/greeting/hello.wav at 16,000 Hz. SHA-256: 87306852e02c15f481cc53773a3f28d1722a8dca5318194053fe30300a695c06. Replay this file rather than regenerating speech. Physical speaker device and operating-system volume must be recorded for a future replication; they are unavailable for the original sessions.

Configuration tolerances specify a 50 Hz feedback-poll target, minimum 20 Hz, ±3° excursion tolerance, ±2° neutral/head rotation/base yaw tolerance, 2 mm head translation tolerance, and ±50 ms timing tolerance. These are engineering targets, not achieved measurements in the submitted participant data. Initial commissioning used half amplitudes of 5° and 15° in the protocol; those values are not participant conditions.
