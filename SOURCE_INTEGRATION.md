# Source integration map — antenna-greeting-v1

Potential final project: expressive greetings that communicate intended social impressions. This lab tests perceived affect and sociability, without assuming that Reachy experiences emotion.

| Source app and fixed revision | Verified reused component | Active integration and purpose | Deliberate modification |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [Reachy Mini Conversation App](https://github.com/pollen-robotics/reachy_mini_conversation_app/tree/b9f58a3587d79a3275d4402d1a8d210649d62d50), Apache-2.0 | `src/reachy_mini_conversation_app/streaming.py::audio_to_float32` | `conversation_audio.read_wav` calls the ported converter on the same frozen PCM16 greeting before `AudioOutput` schedules speaker playback in every A/B trial. Consistent sample conversion preserves the common audible greeting. | WAV loading, fixed audio selection, preopened output and synchronization are custom. No microphone, LLM, live response, or entire Conversation App is invoked. |
| [Pollen Robotics Emotions](https://huggingface.co/spaces/pollen-robotics/emotions/tree/cde39d23da61ada61d9ae4635bee051334434b44), README-declared MIT | `emotionsLibrary.ts` trajectory parsing/decimation; `trajectoryPlayer.ts::computeInitialGotoDuration` and `headMagicMm` | `greeting.compile_greeting` runs the ported parser/decimator; `SDKRobot.neutral` uses the ported distance-based transition policy to enter/recover neutral before and after every cycle, including interrupted trials. | Custom antenna-only minimum-jerk trajectory and Python SDK transport. Recovery adds a 0.5 s floor, body return and feedback checks; it lies outside the six-second stimulus. No emotion-library performance, wheel UI, WebRTC player, or companion sound is used. |

Flow: frozen WAV → Conversation PCM converter → custom scheduled output; custom amplitude trajectory → Emotions parser → single SDK scheduler → measured joint feedback; stop/end → Emotions transition calculation → custom neutral recovery verification.

Participant interaction: the participant says “Hello” first, then the facilitator uses the operator trigger to start the already prepared “Hello, nice to meet you” response with the assigned antenna movement. The six-second cycle covers the robot response, excluding participant speech and operator delay. This human-initiated exchange uses fixed playback rather than automatic speech recognition or live response generation.

These are two **app components**, not two complete running apps. The PCM converter is a small component; no claim is made to live conversation integration. The greeting trajectory, scheduler, measurements, study logic and analysis are our implementation. The reused code has a real execution path, not merely similar functionality. Removing the converter breaks the audio input path; removing the Emotions ports removes the trajectory ingestion and neutral-transition calculations.
